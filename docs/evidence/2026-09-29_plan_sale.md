## 5. Found during my own E2E check (not the independent review): fixed
- Two card cross-modal cosmetic bugs, found by driving the real Simulator in headless Chrome and reading the
  host's own `crossModal()` self-report (`state.card.entry.crossModal`): (a) the sell-day card's single-house date
  line was missing `data-xm-skip`, leaking the day-of-month into the "on card but not in result" list; (b) the
  Simulator's shared number-extraction regex (`app.js`, pre-existing, also used by `tests/crossmodal.py`'s intent)
  mis-split a negative, comma-grouped rupee figure (e.g. "₹-42,630") into a bogus extra token ("630"), because the
  regex's negative-lookbehind only excluded a source starting right at the sign, not the comma-continued group
  after it — invisible until a tool (this one) actually showed a large negative number. Fixed the regex
  (`(?<![\w.]|[\w.]-)-?\d[\d,]*(?:\.\d+)?`, still excludes ID-like tokens such as "house-2"). Verified: real
  Simulator turns for house 1 (large positive net), house 3 independent (large negative net) and house 3 contract
  (before/after saving terms) all report `crossModal.ok: true`, `unbacked: []`, `missing: []`.

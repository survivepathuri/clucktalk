# From prototype to a service a farmer can depend on

An honest list of what is missing. **Every item below is not done.** The order is a suggestion, not a schedule, and no date or cost is promised. What exists today is in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/RESULTS.md` and the README claims table.

| # | Item | Why it is needed | Where we are | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Alexa+ add-on packaging.** An `addon.json` that Amazon accepts, real icons and a carousel image, a listing with its policy and privacy URLs, and a deployed add-on. | Without it no Alexa+ device can load the server. | `docs/addon-package/addon.json` is a placeholder draft that follows the documented field names and limits (`docs/addon-package/NOTE.md`). Not deployed; the Alexa AI CLI was not used (FL-024); the Local Inspector was not run. | **Not done** |
| 2 | **OAuth 2.1 with PKCE and Protected Resource Metadata** for account linking. | The documented authentication path for Alexa+ add-ons. Today the server uses one bearer token off localhost, which is not account linking. | Not built (`docs/ALEXA_READINESS.md`, "not checkable locally"). | **Not done** |
| 3 | **Remote HTTPS hosting.** A stable URL, a certificate, monitoring, a backup plan and a host that is not a personal PC. | Alexa+ needs a reachable HTTPS endpoint, and a farmer needs it up when he opens it. | The pilot is planned on the builder's own PC through a free tunnel; it has not started. No cloud deployment exists. | **Not done** |
| 4 | **Latency measured end to end.** From a real client over the network with the real data and, if used, a real model. | Amazon's 500 ms is a round trip. Ours are server times on localhost in mock mode. | `docs/RESULTS.md`, section 5. | **Not done** |
| 5 | **A farmer self-service setup screen.** Add houses, placement dates and his own numbers (his deaths rule, his notes) without the builder editing files. | Today the builder writes the farm file and the rule file by hand; entering rules by voice would also need a new tool. | Files only (`docs/pilot_farm.example.json`, `docs/daily_deaths_rule.example.json`). | **Not done** |
| 6 | **A farmer-own growth target.** A target curve built from his own flocks, or one he agrees to, for his breed and feed. | Pilot houses have no breed and no cited growth curve, so there is no weight, feed or sale target for them. A published table for another breed would mislead. | Weights are refused and plans say there is no verified growth standard. | **Not done** |
| 7 | **A selling plan for pilot houses.** The sale-day plan needs a target and a price source he trusts. | `plan_sale` works for the demo farm only. | Not available for pilot houses (no growth curve). | **Not done** |
| 8 | **Native Telugu review of every farmer-facing string, and a Telugu voice path.** | The Telugu wording is draft. There is no Telugu audio output, and no Telugu voice was tested through Alexa+. | Every new string is pending in `docs/TELUGU_REVIEW.md`. | **Not done** |
| 9 | **Veterinary and agronomic review of the thresholds and the triage signs.** | No vet or farmer has validated them (`docs/SAFETY_MATRIX.md`). | General guidance and heuristics, labelled as such. | **Not done** |
| 10 | **Confirming the farmer's own rule in writing.** "2 per 1000" was relayed, not confirmed. | It changes when a shed is raised to WATCH. | The rule file carries his value; the confirmation is open. | **Not done** |
| 11 | **Per-farmer accounts and data separation.** More than one farmer, each seeing only his own houses. | Today there is one farm per instance, and the state file is the builder's. | Single farm per instance. | **Not done** |
| 12 | **Privacy and data-law checks.** What is stored, for how long, who may read it, consent, deletion on request, and any rule that applies to farm data in India. | A real service holds real people's data. | A one-screen notice and a documented delete step exist for the pilot; no legal review was done. | **Not done** |
| 13 | **Live AI verified end to end.** The triage and briefing agents on a live model through the server, with the cost and quota limits checked. | The live path was tested only through mock models and a few one-off Bedrock calls outside the server. | `docs/AWS_SETUP.md`; FL-014. | **Not done** |
| 14 | **A human security audit and a test of the hosting setup.** | The reviews so far were AI agents and the builder's own tests (`SECURITY.md`). | None. | **Not done** |
| 15 | **A card check at 11 inches and above, and on a real Alexa+ screen.** | The cards were tested only at sizes of 10 inches and below, in the Simulator. | `docs/DESIGN.md` section 15. | **Not done** |
| 16 | **Layer (egg) farming in the voice and card experience.** | The build targets broilers only. | The domain layer models layers; the demo and the cards do not. | **Not done** |
